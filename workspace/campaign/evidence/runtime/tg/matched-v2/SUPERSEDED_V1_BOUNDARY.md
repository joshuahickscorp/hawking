# Matched Llama receipts: v1 boundary

The former `matched/` receipts were non-certifying diagnostic measurements. They
remain represented by Git history, but are not a second live evidence root.

The v1 llama.cpp runner timed GPU evaluation plus argmax without token-ID to
text-piece conversion. Hawking's `generate` timing includes that streamed token
work. The runner was corrected in v2 before any performance promotion.

No v1 TPS ratio may be called parity, surpass, ship, dominance, or moonshot.
Both generations remain blocked until current-context K0 and resource
instrumentation are complete.
