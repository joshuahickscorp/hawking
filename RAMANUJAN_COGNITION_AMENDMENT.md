# Continuum amendment — the discovery loop

**Status: CANONICAL.** Folded into the active goal set (`tools/campaign/continuum_status.py`
`STEPS`) as `RAMANUJAN_COGNITION`, positioned after `TRAIN_LOCAL_FORGE` and before
`BUILD_SEARCH_GOVERNANCE`. Reasoning and the two independent source passes are in
`RAMANUJAN_COGNITION_PROPOSAL.md`.

## What this amendment is for

The specified Ramanujan stack is the correct substrate for **certainty** and not a model of
**cognition**. The verifier loop is not the discovery loop; the architecture as written is
almost all Hardy. This amendment adds the missing half.

**Bound on the claim, kept deliberately:** systems on the consensus stack reached elite
performance with no perceptual organ. They solve *stated* problems. Everything here is
justified on conjecture generation and selection, and must be judged there. If a mechanism
only improves proving, it has not earned its place.

## Admission rule

Every mechanism below carries a falsifier and a **kill condition**. A mechanism that fails
its falsifier is deleted, not tuned. This is what keeps an ideation pass from inflating a
campaign: the additions are gates, not aspirations.

None of these may promote their own output. All admission remains the Tribunal's, all Tier-3
evidence remains a clean Lean replay, and `RAMANUJAN_RESEARCH_AUTHORIZED` stays false.

## The seven, in build order

**C1 — Computational Retina.** Massive cheap computation over object families; a small
self-supervised model trained to predict and compress them. The latent space is the
perceptual organ. Foundational — C2, C3 and C4 stand on it.
*Kill:* cannot co-locate the two sides of a held-out nontrivial identity better than a
first-k-terms baseline.
*Note:* the data-discipline constraint is trivially satisfied here — the system generates
its own corpus, so licensing and contamination do not arise.

**C2 — Significance.** MDL compression gain first, because it works from turn one with no
corpus and no cold start. Map dynamics — connectivity, generativity, obstruction-yield —
blended in as the claim graph fills.
*Permanent term:* an exploration bonus for high-compression claims in sparse regions. This
is the anti-fashion-capture device. A significance model trained on what became central
will otherwise devalue exactly the outsider compressions this project is named after.
*Kill:* known deep theorems do not outrank arbitrary true identities of similar length.

**C3 — Definitional Library Learning.** When the retina finds a recurring unnamed cluster:
characterize it, attempt a Lean definition, and if it typechecks and members satisfy it, the
concept enters the Librarian with a name. The frozen Director's *effective* vocabulary grows
without retraining, and every concept is Lean-checked rather than invented jargon. Gated by
C2 or it becomes definition spam.
*Kill:* hide a known concept, supply only the computation, and it fails to mint an
equivalent. This is Q2 rediscovery moved from results to **concepts**.

**C4 — Obstruction Objects.** Search failures are a free training signal. Cluster them,
predict obstruction class and blocking depth, and promote obstruction classes to objects of
study — sometimes impossibility results in waiting.
*Kill:* obstruction-guided pruning measurably reduces the rate of finding proofs that needed
a non-obvious step. That cost is measured, never assumed away.

**C5 — Dual-Channel Fusion.** Every claim carries a coordinate: computational support ×
formal status. The new object worth naming is *high computational support, Lean-blocked at
an identified lemma* — a localized research target. A computational counterexample against
a claimed proof is a defect in the system, and reproduction outranks any proof-search
confidence, exactly as MOP's `adjudicate` already enforces.
*Kill:* the coordinate merely relabels the known open problems.

**C6 — Analogy as Partial Structure Maps.** Relational schemas extracted from Lean types;
partial morphisms maximizing preserved relations; strategies and obstructions transported
along the morphism, then repaired and verified normally. A broken morphism yields an
obstruction object.
*Kill:* gains appear only when source and target are textual near-duplicates — it has
collapsed into retrieval and should be deleted.

**C7 — Competence Cartography.** A per-domain, per-complexity map of where the system's own
perception is reliable, used for routing and budget.
*Hard constraint:* measured externally on held-out per-domain benchmarks, never introspected.
Producer and verifier stay separate even when the subject is the system itself.
*Kill:* declared competence does not predict held-out per-domain performance.

## Dependency on the MOP transfer

Every mechanism here is a new way for the system to fool itself. That raises the value of
porting MOP's `method/` refusal layer — SESOI, multiplicity, stop rules, claim ceilings,
producer/verifier separation, `wording_check`, `adjudicate` — rather than lowering it.
`BUILD_SEARCH_GOVERNANCE` is amended to include that port.

## Ordering constraint

This phase begins only after `HAWKING_EVOLUTION_COMPLETE` — Hawking refactored and sealed,
not shipped — and after `TRAIN_LOCAL_FORGE`. It does not gate, and is not gated by, anything
in the Hawking half of the Continuum.
