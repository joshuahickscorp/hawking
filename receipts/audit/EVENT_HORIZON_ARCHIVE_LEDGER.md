## Sub-1 evidence packet disposition

The eight unreferenced `workspace/campaign/odyssey/evidence/sub1/*.md`
documents were a narrative working packet. The durable conclusions are:

- synthetic or Gaussian activation probes cannot authorize a production
  representation; real, route-conditioned activation evidence is required;
- beating a mean/null baseline is diagnostic, not a quality bar, and centered
  SVD, raw weight-space PQ/VQ, entropy-coded indices, and shared expert weights
  remain killed on their recorded premises;
- activation-weighted, lookup-free factors and shared scale metadata are live
  hypotheses, not qualified product capabilities;
- expert, non-expert, stored, active, and complete-system BPW denominators must
  remain separate; model-specific geometry cannot be promoted as universal;
- the current path is an organ-level screen with absolute held-out floors,
  native fallback, and explicit `UNKNOWN` quality where no real capture exists.

The packet's numerical tables and citations remain in the corresponding
receipts and canonical evidence index. No qualified artifact, measurement,
negative-science scar, or promotion decision was removed.

## Retention rule

Future reductions should preserve only: a current owner, a decisive receipt or
scar, a minimal reproducer when it prevents regression, and the smallest
canonical contract needed to explain why a rejected family stays rejected.
Historical implementations remain recoverable in Git; they do not remain
active merely because they once carried a useful explanation.
