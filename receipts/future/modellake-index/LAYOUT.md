# ModelLake layout

Lake root: `/Volumes/corpdrive/hawking-modellake`

Generated by `tools/odyssey/modellake_index.py`. Do not hand-edit: it is
rewritten from LAYOUT on every index build.

## `specimens/`

External HDD: cold/rolling specimen school and verified source bodies.

Law: sealed verified source bodies; the index never writes, moves, deletes or compresses anything here

## `manifests/`

acquisition manifests beside the bodies they describe; reacquisition recipe lives here. One per body: without a manifest modellake.retire() refuses to relegate a specimen, so a gap here is a body the lake cannot free. manifests/retired/ keeps the recipe for a body deliberately removed, and is not a live manifest.

## `index/`

durable incrementally-updatable catalog. Query this; do not walk 4.35 TB.

## `logs/`

everything the lake writes about itself: acquisition worker logs, acquisition-state.json, and compressed archives of retired campaign logs

## `partial/` *(transient: exists only during an acquisition)*

Partials: quarantined lifecycle objects until verified and atomically promoted.

Law: quarantined until verified and atomically renamed into specimens/

## `claims/` *(transient: exists only during an acquisition)*

filler worker claims (pid/slug/taken_at); not a specimen body
