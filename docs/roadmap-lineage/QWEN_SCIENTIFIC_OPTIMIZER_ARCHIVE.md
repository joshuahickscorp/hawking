was a blocked research observer, not a runtime or HCLI authority. It had no
material physical state in HEAD, no production caller, and its own contract
refused to claim native full-token throughput. The wrapper and dedicated test
fixture were parallel scaffolding, so all three executable surfaces were
retired.

- the optimizer schemas and claim boundary are recorded by the historical
  nomenclature and archaeology receipts;
- its required evidence inputs remain in the Qwen campaign records and
  canonical receipts;
- the decisive rule remains: component I/O profiles are not model TPS, and
  absent native runtime evidence is `BLOCKED_NATIVE_RUNTIME`;
- the complete implementation remains recoverable from Git history.

Future Qwen work belongs in the current physical qualification/HCLI path,
with experiment variation represented as receipts and data rather than a
second detached watcher.
