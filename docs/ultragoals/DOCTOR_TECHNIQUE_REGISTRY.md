## Law

**Literature is a hypothesis, not an authority.** (S026 §5)

A paper result is not Metal performance (S026 §89). A paper result is not a
promotion. Register the mechanism before implementing it (S026 §76). Provenance
is preserved (S026 §88). Blind implementation is forbidden (S026 §6).

**No CURRENT VERDICT other than UNTESTED without a cited Hawking receipt.**
A Hawking receipt is a `receipts/**/*.json` path that exists on disk or in git.
An arXiv URL is not a receipt. A design-lane "not worth building" is a receipt
only when it is itself a Hawking JSON. A test of the generator fails if an
entry claims a measured verdict with no such citation.

UNTESTED is a legal verdict. It requires a cheapest Hawking experiment (CPU,
no second 27B, real activations not Gaussian) so the next lane does not have
to invent one.

These are this campaign's measurements. They seed any paper whose overlapping
mechanism was already run. They do not prove the paper false in its own
setting; they bound what is legal to claim *here*.

| mechanism | receipts | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| shared basis | `SHARED_BASIS_KERNEL`, `SHARED_BASIS_COHERENT` | competent kernel but dead <2.25 |

| low-rank residual | `HYBRID_OPERATOR` | never heals |
| ternary | `BYTES_FRONTIER` | slower + argmax flip |
| sparse residual | `BYTES_FRONTIER` | indices cost more |

Shared basis is not one of the fifteen papers. It is still a closed door
for the K=2 operating point: the fused kernel is competent and the 0.53-bpw
byte win translates to token_ns, and the coherent point still dies below
q2f 2.25 on held-out activations.

C5 (Walsh-Hadamard as the executable operator, `NOT_WORTH_BUILDING`) is
**not** SpinQuant. SpinQuant is an absorbed rotation then a quantizer in
the new frame. That remains UNTESTED; N044 is the discriminator (S026 §78,
§117).

## Reopening

S026 §11: a closed floor reopens only if the coordinate system, representation
family, healing regime, execution operator, accounting, or composition
objective changes. QWEN_MLP_2_25 stays closed for its family. A rotation that
physically moves the barrier may open `QWEN_MLP_ROTATED_TERNARY`. A paper is
not that demonstration.

S026 §63 / §64: a candidate rejected **for a purpose** is not globally dead.
The 1.25 binary body is dead as a promotion target and still a legal *draft*
hypothesis for Medusa/MTP.

## Native execution

Trit-plane, codebook, and sparse families must execute native (S026 §17, §27,
§28). Reconstruct-to-dense is a fail. Sparse only with a competent fused
path (N033 / CSR lesson). Competence gate S026 §90 / N003: condemn the kernel,
not the representation, until the screen passes.

Complete executable closure (S026 §93) counts tokenizer data, codebooks,
rotations, bases, sparse indices, scales, low-rank factors, state metadata,
aux heads, and routing. No fake low EBPW.
