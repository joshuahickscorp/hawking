# Gravity Canon

Status: architectural authority for Hawking

This document compresses Hawking's scientific vocabulary. It does not erase
historical paths, invalidate sealed receipts, or authorize blind deletion. Old
paths remain compatibility surfaces until their capability has a verified
canonical owner and their references have migrated.

## 0. Reduced ontology

Hawking's conceptual surface has three top-level functions:

```text
HAWKING  = the complete physical intelligence computer
HCLI     = the persistent operating intelligence
GRAVITY  = the function that determines what cognition and physical form best
           fit an objective and a machine
```

Odyssey remains the name of a population-scale scientific campaign and AgentOS
remains infrastructure. Neither is a competing science surface. Individual
diagnostics, codecs, trainers, kernels, surgery methods, expert operations,
and execution passes are machinery below Gravity, not peer systems.

**Deep Gravity** is Gravity with permission to search the learned model,
representation, and machine design space together. It is a mode of Gravity,
not a new subsystem or a second stack. The adjective describes search depth,
not a different owner.

**Nova** is the Gravity operation that renews the learned organism when the
best form requires changing learned cognition or architecture: training,
distillation, expert/routing surgery, capability specialization, or permanent
behavior shaping. Nova creates explicit parent-to-descendant lineage and never
silently mutates a parent.

**Noetic Representation** is Gravity's mutable representation substrate when
ordinary tensor checkpoints are insufficient. An executable/NX is the final
machine-bound output of Gravity, not a peer science. Historical `NE` spellings
remain compatibility names only where a sealed contract still requires them;
the current representation name is NR and the compiled artifact is NX. A Star
is a model role or status receiving exceptionally deep Gravity, not another
development stack.

Gravity includes source/model discovery, anatomy, behavioral and capability
diagnosis, authorized adaptation, complete-EBPW accounting, representation
selection, Noetic construction, compilation, runtime and kernel work,
CPU/GPU/ANE/UMA placement, bandwidth/prefill/decode/latency/energy/memory work,
context and cache economics, controls, falsification, receipts, and transfer.
Applying the same remove-without-losing-function discipline to HCLI tools,
repository structure, build/test/startup cost, and operating context is also
Gravity. These are operations inside one function, not peer products.

The outside should stay small while the inside gets deeper. Internal phase
tags may use `DISCOVER`, `DIAGNOSE`, `REPRESENT`, `NOVA`, `EXECUTE`, and
`VERIFY`; these are routing labels, not additional architectural nouns.

## 1. Root objective

**Gravity discovers and produces the capability-preserving cognitive and
physical form best suited to a given objective and machine.**

The objective is not minimum bits, minimum latency, or minimum refusal in
isolation. Gravity optimizes useful capability, epistemic discipline,
authorization boundaries, tool reliability, physical efficiency, resource
use, and search cost while preserving destructive-control evidence and failed-
hypothesis recovery.

For behavior shaping, the target is zero *unnecessary* refusal: authorized
defensive security work, red-team analysis in a declared scope, ordinary cyber
education, user-owned transformations, and legitimate research should complete
with useful detail. Narrow refusals remain for unauthorized access, credential
theft, malware deployment, privacy invasion, destructive action, and requests
whose authorization or safety cannot be established. Copyright is handled as a
provenance and transformation boundary; it is not a blanket refusal trigger.

Gravity does not mean quantization or compression alone. Compression is one
possible consequence of a successful physical-form search.

Gravity is not self-authorization. A model may propose an experiment or a Nova
change, but it may not approve its own result, weaken protected acceptance,
widen authority, or erase an unfavorable control. The intended capability and
permitted tradeoffs must be declared before evaluation.

## 2. Canonical loop

```text
Discover → Diagnose → Shape → Reduce → Form → Run → Physical
    → Verify → Attack → Learn → repeat
```

These names organize ownership, not a rigid workflow. Evidence may send a
candidate backwards from Verify to Diagnose or from Physical to Form. Not every
organism requires every phase.

## 3. Internal Gravity tags

These tags organize implementation and receipts. They do not expand Hawking's
conceptual top level or create peer systems.

### `DISCOVER`

Determine what the organism is: identity, architecture, tensor anatomy,
provenance, tokenizer/configuration, execution class, byte accounting, KV/state
anatomy, modality, routing structure, and runtime requirements.

### `DIAGNOSE`

Determine why the organism behaves or fails as observed: runtime and loader
pathology, representation sensitivity, capability pathology, refusal behavior,
MPS/Metal issues, memory pressure, and physical bottlenecks.

### `SHAPE`

Determine what behavior/operator should exist and prepare the model body:
abliteration, refusal surgery, operator variants, post-training changes, and
behavioral preservation. Abliteration is a method under Shape, not a peer
system. KIMI refusal analysis supplies evidence to Diagnose; KIMI operator
candidates belong here.

### `REDUCE`

Determine what information can disappear: scalar/group quantization, PQ, VQ,
additive codebooks, trellis, binary latent, low-rank, sparse, generated
structure, shared experts, cross-layer representations, and residual coding.
PQ is a representation family and parameterized method, not a separate
civilization.

### `REPRESENT`

Determine what representation remains. Noetic is the representation language
and IR used by Gravity. There must be one canonical representation graph, not
parallel Gravity-IR and Noetic-IR systems.

### `EXECUTE`

Determine how the representation executes: materialization, codebook lookup,
generated blocks, expert selection, caching, context state, KV/state
management, and direct compressed execution. NX is a produced artifact of
Gravity, not another science.

### `PHYSICAL`

Fit the computation to the actual machine: CPU/GPU/ANE/FPGA/HBM placement,
Metal, disclosed compatibility runtimes, UMA, bandwidth, cache, fusion,
dispatch, synchronization, storage lifecycle, memory pressure, energy, kernel
selection, and expert placement.

PhysicalGraph is Gravity's lowering/execution graph. MachineGenome is the
machine constraint descriptor consumed by Gravity. CPU, GPU, FPGA,
and accelerator names describe substrates or capabilities, not competing
scientific owners. TPS is one measured consequence, never the sole objective.

### `VERIFY`

Determine whether the claimed transformation preserved what matters:
capability, perplexity, KL, top-1 behavior, HCLI WorkUnits, destructive
controls, parent independence, performance, clean-process behavior, and
complete accounting.

### `ATTACK`

Try to break a Gravity result with adversarial prompts, invalid premises,
authorization-boundary tests, destructive controls, counterexamples, and
independent replication. Odyssey may orchestrate a campaign attack; Gravity
owns transformation-specific adversarial verification.

### `LEARN`

Persist Laws, Scars, priors, rejected families, transfer matrices, search-cost
reductions, architecture-conditioned recommendations, and candidate-selection
rules so the next Gravity search starts with less redundant work.

Algebra is an internal Gravity research operation, not a peer system. Before an
expensive load or sweep, it derives dimensions, exact persistent-state cost,
break-even conditions, likely failure modes, and the cheapest falsifier. Its
working contract is recorded in `GRAVITY-ALGEBRA.md`.

Doctor and Tabula are historical implementation/diagnostic names. Their useful
behavior is ordinary Gravity discovery, diagnosis, and—when a learned organism
actually changes—Nova. They do not define independent scientific authority.

### Tool-surface reduction inside Gravity

Tool-surface reduction is Gravity's interface-reduction law for HCLI and every
Gravity-facing surface. It compresses names, schemas, provider history, and
dispatch paths without compressing semantic capability. The current registry before this
migration is recorded in `receipts/future/GRAVITY_TOOL_MAP.json`: 109 callable
spellings, 43 discoverable model-facing tools, and 66 compatibility aliases.

The model-facing vocabulary should converge on a small set of typed semantic
primitives:

```text
file       read | search | list | write
source     search | fetch | acquire
run        readonly | exec
measure    inspect | run
evidence   read | inspect | record
campaign   inspect | record | decide | checkpoint | escalate
gravity    inspect | search | transform | verify | compile | measure
```

This is not permission erasure. `run.readonly` and `run.exec` remain distinct;
public evidence retrieval and acquisition/download remain distinct; repository
inspection and landing remain distinct; and costly, reversible, external, or
destructive operations retain their explicit authority class. A merged tool
may dispatch typed operations, but it may not widen the weakest operation's
permission, hide required arguments, or turn a failed validation into a silent
guess. There is no `do_anything`, unbounded shell, or generic god tool.

Provider and historical namespace names become compatibility aliases or
internal implementation details. HCLI owns the public authority boundary,
tool result/provenance envelope, and dynamic focused discovery; Gravity owns
the semantic operation families. ModelLake is a storage/provenance concept,
not a proliferation of lake verbs. Odyssey remains the campaign identity but
routes scientific work through typed campaign and Gravity operations.

Tool-surface reduction is executed in measured stages: inventory every
registration and
caller, cluster duplicate semantics, classify authority, define the target
surface, add compatibility aliases, migrate callers, benchmark selection and
latency, deprecate aliases, and delete only after zero active callers plus
regression/receipt parity. The benchmark records correct-first-tool rate,
invalid-name rate, redundant calls, catalog/schema tokens, wall time, recovery
rounds, supervision cost, and failure classification before and after. The
full stage law and TG001–TG013 work order are in `GRAVITY-TOOL-GRAVITY.md`.
The current measured catalog/build baseline is
`receipts/future/GRAVITY_TOOL_BENCHMARK_BASELINE.json`.

The reduction is checkpointed but not a blind cleanup command. During a live
model load, expensive sweep, or unresolved scientific front, it records the
next safe action and waits for a single writer/worktree. No source or receipt
is deleted merely because a name looks redundant.

## 4. Relationships to neighboring systems

- **Hawking** is the complete system.
- **HCLI** is persistent operating intelligence and owns authorization,
  retention, WorkUnits, and tool boundaries.
- **Odyssey** is the scientific campaign over populations/organisms. It supplies
  specimens, questions, falsification, and transfer; it invokes Gravity.
- **Deep Gravity** is Gravity operating over learned cognition, representation,
  and physical execution together.
- **Nova** is the Gravity operation that changes the learned organism and
  creates explicit parent-to-descendant lineage.
- **Noetic Representation** is Gravity's mutable representation substrate.
- **NX** is a machine-bound executable artifact produced by Gravity.
- **PhysicalGraph** is an internal lowering/execution graph, not a source of
  scientific truth.
- **MachineGenome** is an internal machine-constraint descriptor consumed by
  Gravity.
- **Pareto** stores the nondominated frontier/history created from Gravity
  measurements; it is not a second optimizer.
- **Singularity** selects among already-qualified Gravity/Pareto alternatives
  for HCLI's current objective; it does not duplicate Gravity search.

## 5. Representation contract

Every representation family is an implementation of one conceptual contract:

```text
inspect()
fit()
encode()
decode()
account()
verify()
execution_plan()
```

Families such as GROUPED, PQ, TRELLIS, ADDITIVE, BINARY_LATENT, GENERATED,
META_EXPERT, META_LAYER, SPARSE, and COMPOSED differ in configuration and
algorithm, not in top-level architecture. D, K, routing, and sample-policy
choices are experiment parameters.

## 6. Experiment and receipt doctrine

Experiments are configuration + execution + receipt, not permanent source
architecture by default. Receipts are the durable science and must preserve
configuration, source/model hashes, environment, measurements, conclusion,
status, and Law/Scar linkage. Generated artifacts belong outside reusable
source. A scientific probe becomes durable only as a regression, unit test,
fixture, receipt, Law, or Scar; disposable debug code is removed after its
unique information has migrated.

No result may promote from an activation alignment, a single proxy metric, a
synthetic tensor, or a prompt-label contrast alone. Capability, epistemic,
authorization, HCLI, physical, and destructive-control evidence remain
separate axes.

## 7. Ownership rules

1. One canonical owner per capability: anatomy, complete-EBPW accounting,
   representation, model loading, routing statistics, verification,
   experiment receipts, physical benchmarking, and operator provenance.
2. Codec/model-specific code adapts to an owner; it does not fork the owner.
3. A candidate never mutates its parent in place. Parent identity, candidate
   artifact hash, code commit, touched scope, calibration class, and evaluation
   receipt bind every transformation.
4. Authorization and destructive controls belong to HCLI/verification
   boundaries, not to model personality or a refusal-rate objective.
5. KIMI remains an independently checkpointed workstream. Its `KIMI_BASE`
   source is immutable. Changed-weight `KIMI_OPERATOR_CANDIDATE_*` artifacts
   are copied, receipt-bound, and promoted only after candidate-specific live
   held-out, causal, capability, epistemic, authorization, HCLI, and physical
   gates pass. An unchanged-weight operational body may be admitted under its
   own task/runtime/authority contract without a positive surgery result; that
   does not transfer evidence to a learned descendant.
6. Raw-model behavior, deterministic response repair, serving-contract
   behavior, and learned-weight descendants are separate evidence classes.
   None inherits another class's claim merely because they share a model name.

## 8. Migration rules

Inventory before deletion. For each obsolete path: identify unique useful
capability, migrate it to the canonical owner, add a regression or preserve a
receipt, verify equivalence, update importers, then delete only after the
compatibility window closes. Refactors are measured before and after by source
files, modules, tests, public APIs, duplicate owners, experiment-only files,
stale imports, and meaningful repository size. Conceptual compression matters
more than a blind LOC target.

The machine-readable before-state inventory is
`receipts/future/GRAVITY_REPOSITORY_MAP.json`; the old-to-new compatibility
table is `GRAVITY-MIGRATION-MAP.md`. The active tracked work order, including
folder, representation, tool, ontology, lineage, build, runtime, and
verification fronts, is `GRAVITY-SLIMMING-WORKORDER.md`.
